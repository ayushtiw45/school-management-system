FAQ

Common questions

Where is my school's data saved?

Only on your laptop, in the folder C:\Users\<your name>\AppData\Roaming\TDS School Data. We cannot see your data, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up my data?

Copy the TDS School Data folder to a pen drive or Google Drive once a week. To restore it on a new laptop, install the software, then put the folder back in the same place before opening it.

What happens when the trial or license ends?

Your data is never deleted or locked. The software becomes read-only: you can still view students, print receipts and download reports. To add new admissions or payments, activate a license.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the latest setup from this website and install it over the old one. All your data, settings and license stay as they are.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Internet is only used if you connect an SMS or WhatsApp provider to send messages automatically.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Each license is for one computer. The data of each installation is separate.

Can parents get SMS or WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Fee reminders, payment confirmations and announcements can be sent through your own SMS/WhatsApp provider. Without a provider, use the free WhatsApp button next to each pending student.

How do I close the software?

Just close the browser tab. To stop it completely, use Stop TDS School Management from the Start menu. Your work is saved automatically.
